Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026
  • Your browsing history never leaves your device.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no tracking or analytics.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to us. We never see your data.
  • Delete it from your device with one tap, any time.

What Tabwise does with your data

When you load your Google export, Tabwise opens the file inside your browser on your own device. It reads your Chrome history there and works out the time spent per site and category. The file and the results are never uploaded to any server. Not to us, and not to anyone else.

What's stored, and where

To save you from loading the file every visit, Tabwise keeps a summary (sites, times and categories) in your browser's own storage on that device. It stays there until you delete it. Other websites can't read it, and it isn't synced anywhere.

To remove it: open Tabwise, go to About your data, and tap Delete my data from this phone. Clearing your browser's website data also removes it.

Tabwise also remembers a couple of display settings on your device (like which time range you last looked at). These contain no browsing data.

Your Google export

The "Get my data from Google" button opens Google Takeout, Google's own service for downloading your data. Requesting the export happens entirely between you and Google, under Google's privacy policy. Tabwise never sees your Google account or password.

Other services the page uses

Like most websites, loading Tabwise means your browser contacts a few services. They receive the usual technical details any website gets (such as your IP address and browser type), but never your browsing data:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the Inter typeface. See Google Fonts privacy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) provides the small tool that opens .zip files. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Tabwise doesn't use cookies, ads, analytics, or tracking pixels.

Installing as an app

If you add Tabwise to your home screen, your device saves a copy of the page so it opens quickly and works offline. That copy is the app itself, not your data.

Children

Tabwise doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
